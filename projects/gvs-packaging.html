Project 01

GVS Packaging Process Improvement

Senior Design / Manufacturing Design — Jan 2026 – May 2026

Cut final packaging labor for GVS Kawasaki filters from 5 operators to 2, while holding a 9-second cycle time, by designing custom ergonomic fixtures.

Overall system / line photo
Close-up of the fixture
CAD model / SolidWorks

Motivation

GVS needed to package high volumes of automotive filters without adding headcount, while keeping quality consistent whether the operator was experienced or brand new to the line.

My contribution

  • Designed and 3D-printed ergonomic fixtures, including a pneumatic prefilter installation device and a 20-bag bagging jig
  • Standardized the fixture design in SolidWorks, iterating through multiple prototypes based on fit, friction, and usability data
  • Validated the design to hold a consistent 9-second cycle time across operators of varying experience

Technical details

  • SolidWorks for fixture modeling and design iteration
  • 3D-printed prototypes for rapid physical testing
  • Ergonomic assessment and cycle-time analysis
